About Go-Go Town Guide

Unofficial fan site · Not affiliated with Prideful Sloth

Go-Go Town Guide is a free, unofficial Go-Go Town! site focused on first-week checklists, calm priorities, and “what to skip” advice for new mayors.

Who operates this site

This is an independent fan project. We publish practical English guides for beginners. Contact: see the Contact page.

Editorial approach

  • Guides prioritize decisions and routines over wiki dumps.
  • We use real game screenshots as we play Story Mode.
  • We update pages when major game updates change early advice.
