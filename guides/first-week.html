Go-Go Town First Week Checklist

Updated: September 17, 2026 · Unofficial fan guide

Start in Story Mode, finish the tutorial jobs one at a time, and ignore Creative Mode until you understand the loop.

On this page

  1. Quick answers
  2. How to start
  3. Opening loop
  4. Safe to skip
  5. Screenshots

Quick answers

  1. Pick Story, not Creative Mode, for a real beginner save.
  2. Say yes to the tutorial—it teaches carry, bins, trucks, and workshops.
  3. Press F to talk and E to pick up when prompts appear.
  4. Follow billboard arrows; don’t open every menu on day one.
  5. Verify tip details in your own save and version.

From our first-week save: The calm runs were the ones where we finished one billboard job before chasing the next glowing prompt—talk, carry, load truck—not the runs where we tried to decorate and hire everyone at once.

How to start

  1. New Town → Story mode.
  2. Name the town (English is fine for guide screenshots).
  3. Play the tutorial when asked.
  4. Talk to the Town Group Representative (F).

Opening loop

  1. Read the floating prompt / billboard.
  2. Pick up the marked item (E).
  3. Drop it where the arrow points (bin, hole, or truck bed).
  4. Use Transfer at workshops only when the UI opens.
  5. One job at a time until the tutorial flag clears.

Safe to skip (for now)

  • Creative Mode “everything unlocked” runs for learning basics
  • Online join / co-op before you know solo controls
  • Maxing every decoration while crates are still on the ground
