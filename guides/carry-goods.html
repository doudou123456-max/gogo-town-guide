Carry Goods Without Panic

Updated: September 17, 2026 · Unofficial fan guide

Quick answers

  1. Stand on the highlighted item until you see E · Pick up.
  2. Walk to the green bin / drop point shown on the billboard.
  3. Interact again to deposit—don't hunt random machines first.

Wood into the bin

When the board shows a log arcing into a crate, that is your whole job: pick every piece on the green mat, then fill the bin beside it.
