Go-Go Town! First Week Guide: What to Actually Do in Your Opening Days

Unofficial fan guide · Updated September 17, 2026

If you just booted up Go-Go Town! for the first time and you’re standing in a half-built lot wondering what you’re supposed to do with a pile of wood and an NPC in sunglasses, welcome. This guide is for that stage — the first few in-game days, while you’re still learning the loop before the game turns into full city-builder chaos.

Go-Go Town! (from developer Prideful Sloth) puts you in as mayor of a fixer-upper town. You’re juggling tourists who wander through and spend Coins, Townies who move in and work for you, and a research currency called EGO that unlocks new tech and buildings. It feels like a lot at once, but the opening hours are mostly about nailing one small production loop before you expand. Here’s how to get through week one without wasting time.

Complete the Tutorial First — Don't Skip It

I know the instinct is to click past tutorial prompts, but don’t. The intro teaches the interactions you’ll use constantly: foraging, crafting, placing props, inviting Townies, spending EGO Tech, and scheduling challenges from your in-game phone. Press TAB to open the phone anytime — that’s your hub for quests, blueprints, and town stats, and you’ll keep checking it.

Your basic interact button is F. You’ll use it on NPCs for dialogue, on resource nodes to pick things up, and on shelves or storage bins to stock and retrieve items by hand before you have staff.

Talk to the Town Representative

Early on you’ll meet an NPC labeled the Town Group Representative. Talking to them (F) is usually how the game hands you the next objective marker. Don’t ignore those prompts — they walk you through systems in order, instead of dumping you into city planning blind.

Gather Wood and Basic Resources

One of your first jobs is manual gathering — walking around and picking up loose wood with F. It’s slow, a bit dull, and still how you stockpile what early construction and crafting need. Don’t grab one piece and leave. Sweep the whole area. Early resources are the bottleneck for almost everything you want to build in week one.

You’ll see the same pattern on other nodes later (Seed Bushes work similarly once farming unlocks): foraging once doesn’t mean you got everything useful. Circle back and forage again, especially early when each pass gives you a little.

Get Your Storage and Crafting Station Set Up

Once you have materials, you need somewhere to put them. Storage crates keep items off your inventory, and crafting stations (like the early workshop) turn raw stuff into something usable. The transfer menu is simple — move items between your backpack and nearby storage — but get used to it early. You’ll shuffle inventory constantly as you grow.

Place storage and your crafting station close together. Sounds obvious, but a lot of new mayors spread early buildings out for looks and then waste time walking. Tight logistics beat pretty layouts in week one — I learned that the hard way after putting my first crafting station across the map from storage and burning an embarrassing amount of time just walking.

Load the Truck and Learn Transport

You’ll get access to a truck fairly early, and that’s your first real taste of town logistics. Goods move by truck instead of you hauling every crate on foot. Learn the loading prompt now — as the town grows, walking every item across the map stops working. Vehicles (and later couriers) keep production moving without you babysitting every delivery.

Build Your First Shop and Keep It Stocked

At the start you’re limited to a few shop types — often something like a general craft shop and a food stall. Pick one, build it, connect it to the road so tourists can reach it, and stock shelves yourself with F until you can hire a worker. Workers auto-fill shelves and save a ton of time, but you won’t have the Coins on day one, so expect manual restocking for a while. When you’re ready to go deeper on layouts and stock flow, see our Go-Go Town! Shop Building Guide (coming soon).

Don't Rush Your EGO Tech Spending

EGO is your blue research currency, separate from Coins, and it’s easy to dump into the first shiny unlock you see. Don’t. Skip early decorative packs — I did that on my first save and had to grind Coins back just to afford a tech upgrade I actually needed. Finish challenge hints first; they teach mechanics that pay off more than cosmetics. Then spend on tech that expands systems — like resource zone upgrades — not décor. For unlock order later, bookmark the Go-Go Town! EGO Tech Unlock Guide (coming soon).

Watch the Weather

Watch for a “golden hour” event after rainy days — sunbeams, a distinctive aura sound, and coins raining across town on a timer. When that hits, drop what you’re doing and collect. It’s free money and it doesn’t last long.

Quick Week-One Checklist

  • Finish the full tutorial before touching anything else
  • Talk to every NPC with a dialogue prompt for quest markers
  • Forage resource nodes repeatedly, not just once
  • Set up storage and a crafting station near each other
  • Get your first shop built, road-connected, and stocked
  • Save EGO for challenge rewards before splurging on tech
  • Chase golden hour weather events for bonus Coins

Wrapping Up

Week one in Go-Go Town! isn’t about building an empire — it’s about learning gather, craft, stock, sell, repeat. Lock in one working loop before you try three shops at once, and the rest of the game opens up more smoothly. Take it slow, follow the quest markers, and don’t be afraid to circle back to resource nodes more than once.
